### 1.1 (IFAC 2026) Vector-Space Optimization for Contraction Theory-Based Control Design: An Energy-Based Effective Space Approach
Summary:
- LMI formulation to vector-space optimization problem.
- Convex input saturation constraints are considered.
- Energy-based constraints are imposed to maintain sufficient control input effort.
Contributions are listed as follows:
- Contraction metrics are calculated by vector-space optimization problem rather than matrix-space optimization problem which depends on system dimension quadratically.
- Using high flexibility of  vector-value optimization formulation, convex input saturation constraints are incorporated into optimization problem, as well.
- To restrict the infinite number of optimal solutions of contraction metrics into finite number of locally optimal solutions owing to scale-invariance of condition number-based objective function, energy-based constraints are considered.
